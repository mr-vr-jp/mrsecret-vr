Launch and Installation

PCCan't select anything in the start screen menu (controllers don't respond)

If the folder path contains spaces or non-English characters such as Japanese

  • Move the game folder to a path with only letters and numbers. The whole path, including parent folders, must be alphanumeric.
OKC:\Games\MRsecretVR

If you renamed the folder after launching the game from it

  • Move the game folder to a completely different location (e.g. Downloads → Desktop).
  • Or rename the folder back to what it was at first launch (e.g. MRsecretVR_ver1.0.1 → MRsecretVR_ver1.0).

If the problem persists

  • Once the menu becomes unselectable, you must restart SteamVR after applying a fix.
  • Download the ZIP file again and extract it to a new location.

Note: These issues are caused by how SteamVR works and can't be controlled by the game.

PCWon't launch in SteamVR
  1. In the SteamVR window, open the three-line menu and select "Settings"
  2. Under OpenXR, select "SET STEAMVR AS OPENXR RUNTIME"
PCWon't launch in SteamVR over Meta Horizon Link (formerly Quest Link)

Change the OpenXR setting in the Meta Horizon Link app (formerly Meta Quest Link) on your PC.

  1. Settings → General → OpenXR Runtime
  2. Set it as active
QuestCan't install the APK file

We've confirmed that installing with SideQuest can fail on Quest 2.

Try "B. Quest Files App" in the Install Guide.

Controls and Gameplay

Can't change her hairstyle

Outfits and hairstyles work slightly differently.

  • Outfits: Take one out of the backpack and just grab it to change her.
  • Hairstyles: After taking one out of the backpack, you need to touch it to her hair.
Can't take off her underwear

You can grab her panties and bra with the grip button.

Doors and items are hard to handle
  • Your hands have physics, so it's easier to put your hand on a door and slide it.
  • Pushing directly with your hand, without the grip button, often works better.
Hand-holding doesn't work well

Grab her between the hand and elbow (the forearm).

Can't hold hands in MR Mode

It works only once after selecting "ポーズリセット" (reset pose) in the menu. This is a limitation of the game's design.

Not sure how to use MR Mode

Open "MR Setup" in the Play Guide

Spatial recognition doesn't work on Quest 3/3S

This feature is for Quest 3 and Quest 3S only. Complete the initial setup in MR Setup, then check the following.

  • "Manage spaces" and your room name appear in Space setup
  • In Space setup, "App permissions" → "Spatial data" → "MRsecretVR" is ON
  • During space setup, a mesh tracing the shape of your room appears
  • You've pressed "空間認識未対応" (spatial recognition off) in the menu to turn it ON
