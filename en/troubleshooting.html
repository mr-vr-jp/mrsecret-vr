Launch and Installation

PCCan't select anything in the start screen menu (controllers don't respond)

If you use the full version, download ver1.2.0 or later from the store where you purchased it. This update fixes controller input issues and launching from folder paths containing Japanese characters or spaces.

  1. Close the game and SteamVR
  2. Follow the PCVR update instructions
  3. Start SteamVR, then run MRsecretVR.exe from the new game folder

If the issue continues after updating, tell us your VR headset, connection method, game version, and symptoms on our Ci-en page.

QuestThe game won't launch and shows “Not responding”

Full ver1.2.0 includes a fix for the standalone Quest game becoming unresponsive at startup. Download the latest version from the store where you purchased it and follow the Quest update instructions.

Save data cannot be transferred when moving from the old store release to this ver1.2.0 release for the first time. Read the update instructions before proceeding.

PCWon't launch in SteamVR
  1. Close the game and check the headset connection steps.
  2. Start SteamVR and confirm that it recognizes your headset and controllers.
  3. Run MRsecretVR.exe from the new game folder.
PCWon't launch in SteamVR over Meta Horizon Link (formerly Quest Link)
  1. Connect to your PC through Meta Horizon Link and confirm that the Link screen appears in your headset.
  2. Start SteamVR on the PC and confirm that it recognizes your headset and controllers.
  3. Run MRsecretVR.exe.

If Link itself cannot connect, check the connection guide.

QuestCan't install the APK file

If updating from the old store release to ver1.2.0 for the first time, read the update steps and save data warning first.

For a failed fresh installation, make sure you extracted the zip and are using the APK inside. If installation through the Quest Files app fails, check developer mode and the SideQuest steps.

Controls and Gameplay

Can't change her hairstyle

Outfits and hairstyles work slightly differently.

  • Outfits: Take one out of the backpack and hold it against her body.
  • Hairstyles: After taking one out of the backpack, you need to touch it to her hair.
Can't take off her underwear

You can grab her panties and bra with the grip button.

Doors and items are hard to handle
  • Your hands have physics, so it's easier to put your hand on a door and slide it.
  • Pushing directly with your hand, without the grip button, often works better.
Hand-holding doesn't work well

Grab her between the hand and elbow (the forearm).

Can't hold hands in MR Mode

If she does not move when you hold her hand, select "ポーズリセット" (Reset Pose) in the menu, then grab her forearm again.

Not sure how to use MR Mode

Open "MR Setup" in the Play Guide

Spatial recognition doesn't work on Quest 3/3S

This feature is for Quest 3 and Quest 3S only. Complete the initial setup in MR Setup, then check the following.

  • "Manage spaces" and your room name appear in Space setup
  • In Space setup, "App permissions" → "Spatial data" → "MRsecretVR" is ON
  • During space setup, a mesh tracing the shape of your room appears
  • After changing room settings or permissions, restart the game and turn on spatial recognition (空間Mesh) once the room has loaded

"非対応" (not supported) can also mean the room mesh has not loaded. Pressing that label alone will not enable it.
