Controls

Basic Controls

Adjust Eye Height

Hold both triggers and move the controllers up or down.

Item Storage

Backpack

  • Reach behind your back and grip to grab the backpack
  • Bring it in front of you to see what's inside
  • 12 storage slots, plus 1 slot on each side

Holster (left wrist)

  • Holds one item you use often
  • Put the monitor in it to watch the feed at all times

Moving Things

Doors, Drawers and Closets

  • Doors: turn the handle and pull/push
  • Drawers: grab the knob and pull toward you
  • Closets: put your hand on the door to open or close it

Doors and drawers are often easier to push/pull directly with your hand, without gripping

Special Interactions

  • Panties: grip and pull down
  • Bra: grip and shift it aside

Motion Sickness Setting

Open "酔い設定" (comfort settings) in the menu to adjust these settings separately.

  • Vignette: adjust how strongly the edges of your view darken while moving.
  • Crouching and turning: choose instant changes or smooth movement.

MR Setup for PCVR

In MR Mode, the background becomes a green space. Virtual Desktop keys out the green and replaces it with your real surroundings.

  1. Open the STREAMING menu
  2. Under "VR Passthrough", turn on "Environment"
  3. Click the Config button on the right
  4. Enter the recommended values

Spatial Recognition Setup on Quest 3/3S

  1. Open space setup in the Quest settings and register the shape of your room.
  2. Allow MRsecretVR to access spatial data.
  3. Start the game and open MR Mode.
  4. Once the room has loaded, turn on spatial recognition (空間Mesh) in the menu.

The room shape briefly becomes visible when enabled. If the menu says "非対応" (not supported), see the spatial recognition checks.

Preventing Motion Sickness

  • Adjust the vignette strength and crouching/turning motion in comfort settings

  • Take a break when you feel tired

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Gameplay

I keep getting caught in Story Mode
  • Always check her position on the monitor
  • She can spot you even through glass
  • Approach from behind while staying out of her view, and watch for her turning around or making contact
  • Use the narrow paths around the house
  • Moving while crouched makes you harder to spot
I don't know how to clear the game
  • When the coast is clear, try opening every shelf and drawer
  • Read the messages that appear carefully
  • Items that show a message when you pick them up can be used somewhere

Controls

How do I take off her panties?
  1. Grab her panties with the grip button
  2. Pull them straight down
Doors are hard to open
  • Push/pull directly with your hand, without gripping
  • Use a turning motion centered on the handle
  • Slide sliding doors left or right
